Talking Points — MB Team Discussion Phase 1

One Audience. One Message. One Campaign.

We have a cannabis list of approximately 2,000 warm contacts. That is our entire Phase 1 audience. We are not splitting it. We are not testing multiple messages. We run one focused campaign to one group, learn what converts, and use that data and revenue to fund Phase 2.

Phase 1 Is Not the Full Vision — It Funds It

Biohackers, Boomers, athletes, and executives are real opportunities — but they require separate lists, separate budgets, and separate strategies we don't have yet. Phase 1 generates the revenue, testimonials, and credibility that open those doors. We build Phase 2 with Phase 1 money.

The Offer Is Simple

Free sample plus $4.95 shipping gets the product in their hands and captures the email. Then a 30-day supply at $44.95. Then a monthly subscription at $34.95. Klaviyo handles the conversion sequence automatically — no manual follow-up required after setup.

Associates Get 20% — Zero Budget to Activate

Each cannabis associate gets their own landing page and pre-written email templates. We only pay commission when a sale converts. This is zero-cost customer acquisition until the moment the sale happens.

The One Metric That Matters at Every Tier

Regardless of list size, the winner of any test is always the offer that delivers the lowest cost per active subscriber — not the most initial purchases. A free sample buyer who subscribes is worth more than a fee buyer who doesn't.

Section 03

Phase 1 Audience Phase 1

Who We Are Reaching in Phase 1

Cannabis users. Warm list of approximately 2,000 contacts through cannabis associates — primarily the Cookies network and related. This audience skews 25–40, culturally forward, and already wellness-oriented. They are comfortable with body optimization, open to enhancement products, and spend money on feeling good. They do not need to be convinced that supplements work — they need to be convinced this one is worth trying.

What We Know About Them

  • Already spend on wellness and enhancement products
  • Comfortable with alternative health approaches
  • Responsive to community-driven recommendations
  • Skeptical of corporate health brands — authenticity matters
  • Mobile-first — they are on their phones
  • Trust the associate who sends the recommendation

What We Do Not Know Yet

  • Exact conversion rate for MB within this audience
  • Which message angle lands best
  • Price sensitivity at $44.95 for a 30-day supply
  • Subscription attachment rate
  • Which associates have the most engaged lists

Phase 1 answers all of these questions.

Phase 2 Audiences — Not Yet Phase 2

Biohackers, Boomers, athletes, executives, and women in perimenopause are strong future audiences for MB — but each requires its own list, its own message, and its own channel strategy. None of those exist today. Phase 2 builds them using Phase 1 revenue and learnings.

The Phase 1 Message Phase 1

"Clean enhancement for your routine. Stack it. Feel sharper in 72 hours."

Lead With

Enhancement and optimization — language this audience already uses. They are not afraid of stacking products. They want something clean that works with their body, not against it.

Avoid

Medical claims. Disease language. Anything that sounds pharmaceutical or clinical. This audience trusts wellness — not prescriptions. Keep it experiential and outcome-based.

Science Role

Available but not the lead. Put the research on the science page for those who look. The homepage and emails lead with feeling and outcome — not mechanism.
