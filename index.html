Section 02

The Offer Architecture

Tier Offer Price Goal
1 — Entry Free Sample Pack + Shipping $0 + $4.95 S&H Get the email. Get the product in their hands.
2 — Core 30-Day Supply $44.95 First real purchase. Establishes the habit.
3 — Recurring Monthly Subscription $34.95/month Recurring revenue. The business model.

Why Free + Shipping Works

  • Buyer self-qualifies — anyone paying $4.95 shipping is a real prospect
  • Credit card captured = email captured = Klaviyo activated
  • Cost per lead drops dramatically vs. paid ads
  • Product in their hands = the best salesperson you have

Sample Pack Contents

  • 5–7 day supply — enough to feel something
  • Not enough to finish — the gap creates conversion urgency
  • Include a card with the 30-day offer and a QR code
  • The moment the sample runs out is when the sale happens
Section 03

Associate Affiliate Program

Commission Structure

20%

Straight commission on every sale — sample, 30-day, and subscription — generated through their unique link.

Individual Landing Pages

Each associate gets a dedicated Shopify landing page with a unique tracking URL. This lets you:

  • Track exactly who is converting
  • Optimize the message per audience
  • Give associates ownership — they promote more

Two Associate Options

Option A — They Email Their List
  • Associate controls the relationship
  • You provide 3 pre-written email templates
  • They send, you get the leads at checkout
  • Best for associates with engaged audiences
Option B — They Hand Over Contacts
  • You email on their behalf
  • Same 20% commission
  • More control over messaging and timing
  • Best for associates with less time

Associate Onboarding Kit (Build Once, Use Forever)

3 Email Templates

Pre-written, ready to send as-is. Each one leads with a different angle: energy, clarity, aging.

Unique Landing Page URL

Their page, their link, their tracking. Managed in Shopify with UpPromote or Refersion.

Product One-Pager

Key talking points, what it is, what it does, what to say and what NOT to say (FDA compliance).

Recommended Shopify Affiliate App: UpPromote or Refersion — both under $30/month and integrate directly with Klaviyo for automated commission tracking.

Open Decisions — Needed Before Launch

These must be resolved at the next GFT1 meeting before any launch commitments are made.

🏷️

Brand Name

BlueVero or something else? Name drives domain, packaging, and all creative assets. Decision blocks everything downstream.

💊

Product Names

MB-C and MB-CL are placeholders. Consumer-facing names needed before packaging and landing pages can be finalized.

💰

Confirmed Price Points

Sample + 30-day + subscription. Prices shown in this plan are recommended — need Kent and team sign-off.

📧

Associate Option A vs. B

Do associates email their own lists, or hand over contacts? This determines who controls the first touchpoint and impacts deliverability.

⚖️

FDA Compliance Review

The regulatory risk on MB as a supplement is high. Legal sign-off is required before any claims go live on the site, in email, or on packaging. This is not optional.

🔬

Lab Confirmation — MB-CL

Can the lab produce Leuco MB specifically? Can they produce effervescent fizz format? "No staining" claim requires Leuco form — standard MB does not deliver this.
