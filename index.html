The Strategic Logic

The cannabis associate network (Cookies and related) skews 25–40, wellness-oriented, and already open to enhancement and optimization. Biohackers are actively searching for MB right now. These audiences don't need to be educated — they need to be handed the right product. Phase 1 captures fast revenue and builds the social proof that unlocks the Boomer market in Phase 2.

Section 04

Associate Affiliate Program Phase 1

Commission Structure

20%

Straight commission on every sale generated through their unique link — sample, 30-day supply, and subscription.

Starting Point

  • Cannabis associate network — Cookies and related
  • ~2,000 warm contacts available at launch
  • Already wellness and optimization-minded
  • No paid acquisition needed to activate this channel
  • Each associate gets a dedicated landing page

Two Associate Options

Option A — They Email Their List
  • Associate sends using your pre-written templates
  • They control the relationship
  • Email captured at checkout — enters your Klaviyo sequence
  • Best for associates with engaged, active audiences
Option B — They Hand Over Contacts
  • You email on their behalf
  • Same 20% commission
  • Full control over message and timing
  • Best for associates with less bandwidth

Associate Onboarding Kit

3 Email Templates

Pre-written, ready to send. Each leads with a different angle: cognitive edge, clean energy, performance stack.

Unique Landing Page

Their page, their link, their tracking — managed via UpPromote in Shopify.

Compliance One-Pager

What to say and what NOT to say. FDA claim boundaries clearly outlined. Protects GFT1 and the associate.

Recommended Shopify Affiliate App: UpPromote or Refersion — under $30/month, integrates directly with Klaviyo for automated commission tracking and associate dashboards.

Section 10

Open Decisions — Required Before Launch

These must be resolved at the next GFT1 meeting. Each one blocks a downstream deliverable. The faster these are decided, the faster Phase 1 launches.
🏷️

Brand Name

BlueVero or something else? Name drives domain, packaging, and all creative. Blocks everything downstream.

💊

Product Names — MB-C and MB-CL

Consumer-facing names needed before packaging and landing pages can be finalized.

💰

Confirmed Price Points

Sample + 30-day + subscription. Prices in this plan are recommended — need GFT1 team sign-off.

📧

Associate Option A vs. B

Do associates email their own lists or hand over contacts? Determines who controls the first touchpoint.

⚖️

FDA Compliance Review

MB carries high regulatory risk as a supplement. Legal review required before any claims go live on the site, in email, or on packaging. This is not optional — it protects GFT1 from enforcement action.

🔬

Lab Confirmation — MB-CL (Leuco)

Can the lab produce Leuco MB specifically? The "no staining" claim requires Leuco form. Standard MB does not deliver this. MB-C launches first. MB-CL launches only after lab confirmation.
